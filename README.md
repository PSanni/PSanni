## 📄 Research Publications

### From Keywords to Concepts: A Late Interaction Approach to Semantic Product Search on IKEA.com

**ACM SIGIR 2025**

We introduce a late-interaction semantic retrieval system for
large-scale e-commerce search, leveraging late interaction models
to improve retrieval quality while maintaining
low search latency.

Our work incorporates LLM-generated synthetic training queries,
negative sampling strategies, and adaptive ranking thresholds.

In live A/B testing on long-tail queries on IKEA.com in the US
market, our approach achieved:

- **+3.10%** Click-through rate
- **+1.96%** Conversion rate
- **+1.78%** Search interaction rate
- **+2.18%** Add-to-cart actions

🔗 [Read the paper — ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3726302.3731948)
